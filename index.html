The Person Behind It

THOMAS JUNG

UI-Centric Art Director · Art Manager · Senior UI Designer

Thomas Jung is a UI-centric art director with a career defined by shipped product — 14 released titles on Sony PlayStation, full UI systems for golf simulators, and brand work across studios and agencies.

He bridges the gap between creative vision and technical execution: designing in Figma with advanced interactive prototyping, then guiding engineering teams through Unity 3D implementation with precision.

Previously Studio Art Director at Full Swing Golf, Creative/Art Director at Phi Tee Golf, and Senior Lead UI Artist at Sony PlayStation for the MLB The Show franchise.
